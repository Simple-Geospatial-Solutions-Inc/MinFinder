# Product

## Register

product

## Users
A mixed field audience in British Columbia. Professional prospectors and exploration geologists sit alongside hobbyists: rockhounds, gold panners, hikers and backcountry travellers who come across old workings. They use the app outdoors, often in bright sun or rain, sometimes wearing gloves, usually with poor or no signal. On the map they want to know what's near them and how to get there. When they add a mine, they're standing at an adit, shaft or pit that MINFILE doesn't list, and they want to record it quickly and correctly before moving on.

## Product Purpose
SGS MinFinder is an offline reference and navigation app for BC MINFILE mineral occurrences, 16,000+ records bundled on the device. Field reports extend it with what users find at those mines: the workings' true location, marked on site; a search that found nothing at the published spot; or a note. Other visitors confirm or dispute them from the site, and SGS reviews new members. Success means users trust what's on the map, a report takes under a minute in the field, and it uploads by itself later.

## Brand Personality
Adventurous, helpful, bold. The app is a capable companion for exploring the backcountry. It is confident and direct, and it takes safety seriously without lecturing. It speaks plainly to hobbyists without talking down to professionals. It should feel like proper field gear, not a web form.

## Anti-references
- Government forms: a long scroll of fields and legal boilerplate.
- Social or gamified apps: badges, streaks, confetti, leaderboards.
- Consumer-cute apps: pastel rounded blobs, mascots, jokey copy.
- Enterprise GIS: dense attribute tables in the style of ArcGIS Field Maps.

The closest positive reference is the Gaia GPS waypoint flow: map-first, drop the pin precisely, then fill in a compact form.

## Design Principles
1. **The map is the ground truth.** Location is the heart of every capture, so show it spatially and never as a bare number.
2. **Field-first ergonomics.** Assume gloves, glare, one hand and no signal. Primary actions are large, high contrast and within thumb reach.
3. **Earn every field.** Keep to what a reviewer actually needs. Optional details stay out of the way until they're wanted.
4. **Honest status.** Always say what state a capture is in (saved, waiting for signal, in review, refused) and why, in plain words.
5. **Safety without sermons.** Say one clear thing about the hazard once, at the moment it matters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.2 AA as a minimum, with extra contrast to cope with glare.
- Status is never shown by colour alone.
- Glove-friendly targets: primary actions at least 48 pt, nothing under 44 pt, and no interaction that depends on precise dragging.
- Layouts survive large Dynamic Type and Android font scaling.
- Every animation respects the OS reduced-motion setting.
