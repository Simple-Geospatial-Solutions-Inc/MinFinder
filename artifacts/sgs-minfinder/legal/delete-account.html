SGS MinFinder — Delete your account

Last updated: 25 September 2026

You only have an SGS MinFinder account if you signed in to send field reports. Browsing the map, offline maps and MinFinder Pro purchases don't need one. Here is how to delete the account and everything it holds.

Delete it in the app (quickest)

  1. Open SGS MinFinder.
  2. Tap the menu in the search bar, then Reports.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. It takes effect straight away.

Don't have the app any more? You can reinstall it, sign in with the same Apple or Google account and follow the steps above.

Or ask us to delete it

Email support@sgss.ca with the subject "Delete my MinFinder account". We don't store your name or email address, so to find your account, tell us whether you signed in with Apple or Google, and which mine you reported on and roughly when. We'll confirm when it's done, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account record (the anonymous Apple or Google identifier we keep);
  • The photos and text of every report you uploaded, including where you stood when you made them;
  • Your confirmations, flags and blocks.

Reports you sent disappear from everyone's app at their next sync. We keep only an empty placeholder for each one — the MINFILE number, its kind and, for a marked working, its pin, with nothing that identifies you — so that devices know to remove it.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: deleted data can remain in our database backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.
  • Copies already shared: field reports that SGS has already shared with the maintainers of public geological records (see our Terms of Use, §7) are not recalled. They were shared without your identity.
  • Reports still on your phone that were never uploaded stay there until you discard them in Reports or uninstall the app.
  • Purchases: MinFinder Pro purchases are held by Apple or Google, not by your account, and are not affected. Manage subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play settings.
