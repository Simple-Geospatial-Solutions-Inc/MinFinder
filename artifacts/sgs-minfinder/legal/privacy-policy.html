SGS MinFinder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

SGS MinFinder is a local-first field tool for exploring British Columbia mineral occurrences. Browsing the map needs no account and keeps your data on your device. The optional Field reports feature lets you sign in and send corrections and notes about MINFILE mines that other people can see; this policy explains what that sends to us, what we publish, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • Browsing, searching, navigating and offline maps need no account. The records, downloaded map tiles and settings live on your device.
  • Your location stays on your device — except when you send a field report from a site or confirm one there, which sends your position to us (see §4).
  • Field reports are optional. To send, confirm or flag them you sign in with Apple or Google. We keep only an anonymous account identifier — never your name or email.
  • Reports you send, with their photos, are published to other users, without your identity. Photos are re-encoded and all photo metadata, including camera GPS, is removed.
  • You can delete your account in the app (menu → My reports → Delete account), which deletes what you uploaded. See how to delete your account.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use advertising networks or analytics/tracking SDKs.
  • Map tiles and field reports come from our own servers in Canada. The optional satellite view streams imagery from Esri. In-app purchases are handled by the app store and RevenueCat.

1. Who we are

SGS MinFinder ("the app") is published by Simple Geospatial Solutions Inc. ("SGS", "we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at support@sgss.ca.

2. What stays on your device

SGS MinFinder is designed to work offline. Unless you use Field reports (§4), the information the app uses is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or to any cloud service. This includes:

  • The MINFILE mineral-occurrence records, which are bundled with the app in a read-only on-device database;
  • Map tiles you pre-download for offline use, saved in the app's private file storage;
  • Your compass calibration offset, map filters and other app settings;
  • Your device's current position, which is used on-device (see §3 and §5);
  • A copy of the public field reports, so they show without a connection.

This data remains under your control. It is kept in the app's on-device database and private file storage, and you can remove all of it by uninstalling the app.

In each case below, only the data described is sent, it is sent to operate the feature you invoked, and — except for map tiles, the update check and field-report sync — it happens because of an action you take. Your continuously tracked position is not sent to us; the only time a position leaves your device is when you send a field report from a site or confirm one there (see §4), or name an offline region (sent to your device's built-in geocoder).

4. Field reports (optional)

Field reports let people correct and update MINFILE mines: mark where the workings really are, say they searched the published spot and found nothing, or leave a note. Other visitors can confirm or dispute what's reported. Reading field reports needs no account. Sending, confirming, flagging and blocking need you to sign in.

Your account

You sign in with Apple or Google. From Apple we request no name or email. Google's sign-in token includes your email address, but our server reads only the account identifier and does not store the email. Our account record holds that identifier, which provider it came from, and when the account was created. Your session is kept in your device's secure keychain or keystore.

What a report you send contains

  • Which mine: its MINFILE number.
  • Location (for "I found it" and "Couldn't find it"): where you placed the pin, where your phone was standing, the GPS accuracy and the time of the GPS fix, and for a search the radius you say you covered. The app refuses to capture when your phone reports a mock (simulated) location. A note carries no location.
  • Photos (optional, taken at the site): up to three, taken with the camera in the app. Our server re-encodes every photo and removes all metadata — camera GPS, device model, serial numbers and timestamps — before storing it. We also keep a short "fingerprint" of each photo to detect the same photo being reused.
  • What you write: the kind of working you marked, and an optional note.

What other people see

A report becomes public once it passes review (below). Other users see its kind, photos, text and date, how far a marked working is from the published location, and how many visitors confirmed or disputed it. For a marked working they also see its pin, which is within 30 m of where you stood. For a search they see the radius you covered, not your position. They never see who sent it: no name, account identifier or other reports by the same person are shown.

Review and moderation

A new account's first reports, and any working marked inside a provincial park, protected area, conservancy, ecological reserve or First Nations reserve, are reviewed by SGS staff before they are shown to anyone else. Reports that members flag are hidden for review, and reports most visitors disagree with are collapsed. Staff may remove content and close accounts that break our Terms of Use.

Confirmations, flags and blocks

  • Confirmations: you can confirm or dispute someone's report only at the site. When you answer, the app records your GPS position and the time of the fix, and sends them when you're next online. Our server uses the position only to check that you were within 75 m of a marked working, or inside the area a search covered, and stores your answer and the time of the fix, not your position. Marking a note "Helpful" sends no position.
  • Flags: we store the reason you chose. The person who sent the report is not told who flagged it.
  • Blocks: private to you. The person you block is not told.

Not signed in yet

You can send or confirm a report with no signal or before signing in. It is kept on your device, with its photos, until you sign in and have a connection, and then uploaded. Until then you can discard a report in the app.

5. Device permissions

The app asks for the following permissions. Data captured through them stays on your device except as described in §3 and §4.

  • Location (while using the app) — to center the map on where you are, to indicate the direction and distance to a selected occurrence, and to record where you were when you send or confirm a field report at a site.
  • Camera (only when you send a field report from a site) — to photograph the working or the ground you searched. The app does not read your photo library.

The app does not use Bluetooth or your contacts.

6. Third-party services

The app relies on the following services. Their handling of any data they receive is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Esri (satellite imagery — contacted only while you have satellite view switched on) — esri.com privacy statement and imagery attribution and terms
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Attest) — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google (Google Sign-In, Play Integrity) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Expo (app update checks) — expo.dev/privacy
  • RevenueCat — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing for in-app purchases

The MINFILE mineral-occurrence data is sourced from the BC Ministry of Energy, Mines and Low Carbon Innovation and is bundled with the app; the app does not send your information to that source to display records. We do not integrate advertising networks, and we do not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.

7. In-app purchases

SGS MinFinder offers optional paid ("MinFinder Pro") features. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed with RevenueCat, which helps us validate purchases and restore them across your devices. To do this, RevenueCat processes purchase and receipt data together with an anonymous app-user identifier it generates; the app does not link this identifier to a name, email, or your Field reports account. We never receive or store your payment card details — those are handled solely by the app store.

8. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or marketing. Reports you send are published to other users as described in §4, without your identity, and SGS may share field reports — marked locations, photos and text, never who sent them — with the maintainers of MINFILE and other public geological records. Otherwise the only sharing is with the service providers listed in §6, strictly to operate the feature you used. We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: the records, downloaded map tiles, settings and cached field reports are kept until you delete the offline data or uninstall the app.
  • Your account and reports: kept until you delete them. Deleting your account (in the app: menu → My reports → Delete account, or see how to delete your account) immediately deletes the photos and text of every report you uploaded, where you stood for each, your confirmations, flags and blocks, and your account record. We keep only an empty placeholder for each deleted report, holding the MINFILE number, its kind and, for a marked working, its pin, and nothing that identifies you, so devices know to remove it.
  • Reports still on your device that were never uploaded are not affected by account deletion; discard them in the app or uninstall it.
  • Backups: our server keeps nightly database backups for 14 days, and we keep off-server copies for up to 30 days, so deleted data may persist in backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten.
  • Server logs: our web server keeps access logs, including IP addresses, for operating and protecting the service. They are rotated by size and not linked to your account.
  • Correspondence: if you contact us at support@sgss.ca, we retain that correspondence for as long as needed to respond to it.

10. Children's privacy

SGS MinFinder is a reference tool intended for geologists, prospectors, and other outdoor and industry professionals. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Field reports accounts are intended for users aged 16 and over.

11. Your privacy rights

We handle personal information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial law. Subject to that law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. You can see and delete your reports and your account yourself in the app. For anything else — or if you no longer have the app — contact support@sgss.ca and we will respond within a reasonable time. Because we don't store your name or email, we may ask you to identify a report (which mine, and roughly when you sent it) so we can find your account.

12. International processing

Our own servers are in Canada. Some of the third-party services listed above operate outside Canada. When a feature sends data to such a service, that data may be processed in other countries under their laws.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be reflected in an updated version distributed with the app.
