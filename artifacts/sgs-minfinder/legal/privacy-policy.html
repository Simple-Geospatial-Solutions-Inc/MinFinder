SGS MinFinder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 July 2026

SGS MinFinder is a local-first field tool for exploring British Columbia MINFILE mineral occurrences. This policy explains what information the app handles, what stays on your device, and the limited cases in which some information is sent to a third party — always so a feature you chose to use can work.

The short version

  • The mineral-occurrence records, downloaded map tiles, and app settings live on your device. There is no MinFinder account and no automatic cloud backup.
  • Your location is used on your device to center the map and point you toward an occurrence — it is not sent to us.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use advertising networks or analytics/tracking SDKs.
  • Maps are drawn with Google Maps and Esri topographic tiles, and in-app purchases are handled by the app store and RevenueCat.
  • You can remove all app data at any time by uninstalling the app.

1. Who we are

SGS MinFinder ("the app") is published by Simple Geospatial Solutions Inc. ("SGS", "we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at support@sgss.ca.

2. What stays on your device

SGS MinFinder is designed to work offline. The information used by the app is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or to any cloud service by default. This includes:

  • The MINFILE mineral-occurrence records, which are bundled with the app in a read-only on-device database;
  • Map tiles you pre-download for offline use, saved in the app's private file storage;
  • Your compass calibration offset and other app settings;
  • Your device's current position, which is used on-device (see §3 and §4).

This data remains under your control. It is kept in the app's on-device database and private file storage, and you can remove all of it by uninstalling the app.

3. Information processed off your device — and when

Certain features need to contact an outside service to work. In each case below, only the data described is sent, it is sent to operate the feature you invoked, and — except for map tiles — it happens because of an action you take. Your location is not in this table: it is used on your device and is not transmitted to us.

FeatureWhat is sentRecipientWhy
Map view The map area you are viewing (which reflects the location you are looking at) Google Maps (map engine) and Esri / ArcGIS (topographic tiles) To render the basemap beneath the occurrence pins
Offline map download The region and zoom levels you choose to save Esri / ArcGIS To fetch the map tiles so they are available offline later
In-app purchases Purchase and receipt information and an anonymous app-user identifier Apple/Google and RevenueCat (see §6) To process the purchase and unlock MinFinder Pro features
Opening a reference link A standard web request when you tap an external link (e.g. the MINFILE or Esri source pages) minfile.gov.bc.ca / esri.com To open the source website you chose to view

4. Device permissions

The app asks for the following permission. Data captured through it stays on your device except as described in §3.

  • Location (while using the app) — to center the map on where you are and to indicate the direction and distance to a selected MINFILE occurrence. Your position is used on-device; it is not sent to us.

The app does not use the camera, photo library, Bluetooth, or your contacts, and it does not create a user account.

5. Third-party services

The app relies on the following services. Their handling of any data they receive is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Esri / ArcGIS (basemap tiles) — esri.com privacy statement
  • RevenueCat — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing for in-app purchases

The MINFILE mineral-occurrence data is sourced from the BC Ministry of Energy, Mines and Low Carbon Innovation and is bundled with the app; the app does not send your information to that source to display records. We do not integrate advertising networks, and we do not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.

6. In-app purchases

SGS MinFinder offers optional paid ("MinFinder Pro") features. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed with RevenueCat, which helps us validate purchases and restore them across your devices. To do this, RevenueCat processes purchase and receipt data together with an anonymous app-user identifier it generates; the app does not link this identifier to a name, email, or account. We never receive or store your payment card details — those are handled solely by the app store.

7. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or marketing. The only sharing that occurs is with the service providers listed in §5, strictly to operate the feature you used. We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

8. Data retention and deletion

The records, downloaded map tiles, and settings used by the app are retained on your device until you delete the offline data or uninstall the app. We do not keep a copy of them. If you contact us at support@sgss.ca, we retain that correspondence for as long as needed to respond to it.

9. Children's privacy

SGS MinFinder is a reference tool intended for geologists, prospectors, and other outdoor and industry professionals. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your privacy rights

We handle personal information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial law. Subject to that law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. Because nearly all of the data the app uses lives only on your device, you can exercise much of this control directly by managing or uninstalling the app. For anything we hold — such as correspondence you sent us — contact support@sgss.ca and we will respond within a reasonable time.

11. International processing

Some of the third-party services listed above operate outside Canada. When a feature sends data to such a service, that data may be processed in other countries under their laws.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be reflected in an updated version distributed with the app.

13. Contact and governing law

Questions, requests, or concerns? Email support@sgss.ca.
